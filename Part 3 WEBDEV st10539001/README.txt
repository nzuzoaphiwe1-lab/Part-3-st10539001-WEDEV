CARs RS - Interactive website demo

Open Index.html in a browser. Keep the HTML pages, style.css, script.js, LOGO.png and your Img folder together.

Added:
- Vehicle search and price/name sorting
- More Info expand/collapse per vehicle
- Purchase button routes to Enquiry.html with the vehicle prefilled
- Required-field validation and confirmation on enquiry
- Enquiry records stored in browser localStorage
- Review submission, star rating and browser localStorage
- Portable relative paths replacing machine-specific Windows paths in HTML

Important:
This is a front-end demonstration. Enquiries and reviews are stored only in the visitor's browser; they are NOT emailed to the dealership or saved to a shared database. For a live business website, connect a secure server/database or form service. Replace placeholder social links and ensure the Img folder/video assets are included.
